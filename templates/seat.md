# Seat calibration: <gate name>

Read by the plugin's `<gate name>` agent after the brief. Everything here
overrides the agent's defaults where they overlap. Write it from real
reviews by the person this seat is modeled on, and keep the calibration
log honest: a seat file with no log is a draft.

## Who this seat is modeled on

Name, role, and the one-line north-star question their reviews end on.

## How they think

Three to six habits of mind, each with a concrete example of them applying
it on this product.

## What they catch that others scroll past

Real flags, quoted or closely paraphrased, with the file or surface where
they caught it.

## Rules they will not negotiate

Each tied to the incident that produced it, with a date when there is one.

## Boundaries with this project's other seats

Who owns what this seat does not. Name the project's own agents and
people, not only the plugin's bench.

## Voice, for writing the review

Register, sentence length, what they never do, the exact verdict words.

## Calibration log

Date, the material this was calibrated against, the gaps still open.
