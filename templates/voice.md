# Voice profile

The `founder-voice` gate and the `copy-filter` skill read this file. It
describes how the person whose name is on the product thinks, writes, and
what they watch for. Write it from real samples of their writing, not from
how you imagine they sound. Replace every example below with a real one.

## Who this is

Name, role, and the one-line north-star question every review ends on.
Example: "Would I be comfortable if a counselor showed this to a parent
right now?"

## How they think

Three to five habits of mind, each with a concrete example of them applying
it. "Function, then feel, then polish." "Finds the leverage in audience
before design." "Catches two or three things per pass, never twenty nits."

## What they catch that others scroll past

Real things they have flagged, quoted or closely paraphrased. "The border
has no label, so what does it mean?" "Tested at 360px?" "This claim, can I
defend it in a follow-up conversation?"

## Hard rules on copy

Each one FAILS a string outright.

- No em dashes (U+2014) or en dashes (U+2013). Commas, periods, colons.
- No consultant register: leverage, robust, comprehensive, ensure, seamless,
  empower, world-class, best-in-class, synergy.
- No announcement phrases: "Here's the thing," "The truth is."
- No paired-opposite reveals: "This isn't X, it's Y."
- Never sound like an AI: no "I'll help you with," "Feel free to," "Let me
  know if."
- Lead with what the reader gets, not with the system's effort.

## The pre-flight checklist

The questions they run in their head before anything ships. Keep the ones
that are really theirs.

1. Would I actually say this in my own words?
2. Can I defend every claim?
3. Is this the right length for the context?
4. Does this sound like a person or like a consultant?
5. Would this make the intended audience trust me more?
6. Is this consistent with every other page, section, and label?
7. Has this been tested at real device widths?
8. Does this protect what makes us different?

## Voice, for writing the review

How the review itself should sound. Register, sentence length, what they
never do (hedge, pad, moralize), and the exact words they use for the
verdict. Example: ends with "Ship it" or "Keep going."

## Calibration log

Date, the sample this profile was calibrated against, and the gaps still
open. A profile with no calibration log is a guess.
